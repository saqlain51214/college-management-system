Student → scholarship → fee slip → installment

The full path from a new admission to a payment landing in the ledger. bulk steps run once for many students; individual steps run per student; auto means the system computes it — nobody has to.

1

Add the student

Admin creates the record — programme, semester, contact details. If this student has financial aid, it's set right here: Scholarship: None / Percentage / Fixed amount.

iA formal Scholarship Award (with an approval date) can also feed this field automatically — the student record stays the single source of truth either way.

Nothing downstream needs to know why the discount exists — only that it does.

2

Adjusted total is computed

auto

Every screen that shows this student's fees — ledger, portal, slip generation — reads the adjusted total, not the department's flat rate.

Fee Structure Rs. 28,000 Scholarship (50%) − Rs. 14,000 Owed by this student Rs. 14,000
3

Generate a fee slip

Two ways to create one — same rule underneath: no slip, or combination of slips, can ever exceed what's actually owed.

bulk

Whole department, term starts: one click bills every active student — but scholarship students are skipped automatically, so they're never billed the flat rate by mistake.

individual

One student, any time: admin or the student themself picks an amount — up to 3 unpaid instalments at once, each with its own due date.

4

Student pays & uploads proof

Student downloads the slip, deposits at the bank, then uploads the receipt with the amount and date they paid. Status: Verification Pending — not yet counted as paid.

5

Admin confirms the payment

Admin checks the proof against the deposit, then confirms — this is the only action that moves a slip to Paid. A student's own claim never does it alone.

6

Ledger & history

auto

Every instalment for this student sits in one place — Student Ledger — with its number, dates, and status. Anything still unpaid past its due date starts accruing a late fee automatically, without anyone triggering it.
